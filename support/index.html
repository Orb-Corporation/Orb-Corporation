WHY SUPPORT?

Build useful things.
Keep them accessible.

Orb's goal is to make practical software available without unnecessary barriers. Contributions are optional and help provide more room for the time and resources involved in maintaining independent projects.

Depending on current needs, support may help with development and testing, documentation, hosting and infrastructure, or the tools needed to keep projects moving.

TRANSPARENCY

Support is voluntary.

No paywall

Donations are not required to use Orb's free-to-use projects.

No promised outcome

A contribution does not guarantee a feature, release date, personal support or a particular result.

Independent development

Support helps sustain the work, but project priorities and technical decisions remain guided by user needs and practical constraints.
