ABOUT ORB CORPORATION

Technology should
work for people.

Orb Corporation is an independent technology venture focused on accessible digital products, open technologies and practical innovation.

OUR MISSION

Make the digital world more accessible.

We aim to create useful software and services that reduce unnecessary barriers to participation in the digital world.

OUR VISION

Build globally.
Start anywhere.

We want to develop ambitious, high-quality technology that is useful to people everywhere and can compete on an international stage.

OUR PRINCIPLES

01

Accessibility

Useful tools should be within reach, not hidden behind unnecessary barriers.

02

Openness

We favour open-source development and transparent practices wherever practical.

03

Independence

We value user choice, interoperability and freedom from avoidable lock-in.

04

Craft

We care about reliability, clear design, documentation and solving real problems.

HOW WE ARE ORGANISED

One parent.
Two distinct roles.

Orb Corporation provides shared direction, identity and long-term coordination.

Orb Technologies develops and maintains user-facing products and services.

Orb Labs explores experimental ideas across software, hardware, computation and creative technology. Its work does not need to become a commercial product to be worthwhile.

These are organisational divisions, not a statement that separate legal entities have been incorporated.
