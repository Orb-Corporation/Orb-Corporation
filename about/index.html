ABOUT ORB CORPORATION

Technology should
work for people.

Orb Corporation is an independent technology venture focused on accessible digital products, open technologies and practical innovation.

OUR MISSION

Make the digital world more accessible.

We aim to create useful software and services that reduce unnecessary barriers to participation in the digital world.

OUR VISION

Build globally.
Start anywhere.

We want to demonstrate that ambitious, high-quality technology can emerge from Türkiye and compete internationally.

OUR PRINCIPLES

01

Accessibility

Useful tools should be within reach, not hidden behind unnecessary barriers.

02

Openness

We favour open-source development and transparent practices wherever practical.

03

Independence

We value user choice, interoperability and freedom from avoidable lock-in.

04

Craft

We care about reliability, clear design, documentation and solving real problems.

SUSTAINABILITY

Access first.
Build to last.

Our direction is to make useful tools free to use and to publish source code openly wherever practical. Long-term development may be supported through voluntary donations, sponsorships and optional services.
